A little closer look at the flowers that colors.

Thong Kwao

Butea monosperma · Fabaceae family

Blooms
December – March
Appearance
Deciduous tree, 10–15m tall, compound leaves with 3 leaflets. Flowers cluster at branch tips, flame-orange to yellow, pea-shaped, covered in soft fuzz.
Meaning
CMU's official flower and Chiang Mai province's official flower. Symbolizes the bond between CMU students and their graduation season.
Found at
Roads and gardens around campus, especially near the royal graduation ceremony period.

Ratchaphruek

Cassia fistula · Fabaceae family

Blooms
March – May
Appearance
Medium-sized tree with bright yellow flowers hanging in long drooping clusters; petals are thin and drop easily.
Meaning
Thailand's national tree and flower. Symbolizes prosperity, honor, and good fortune.
Found at
Main roads and activity plazas across campus.

Sieo

Bauhinia variegata · Fabaceae family

Blooms
December – February
Appearance
Small to medium tree with butterfly-shaped leaves; pale pink-white flowers with 5 thin petals.
Meaning
One of Chiang Mai's representative flowers. Symbolizes gentleness and calm.
Found at
Garden areas and shaded walkways around faculty buildings.

Fueang Fa

Bougainvillea spectabilis · Nyctaginaceae family

Blooms
Nearly year-round
Appearance
Thorny climbing shrub. Vividly colored bracts (purple, pink, red, orange) surround small true flowers at the center.
Meaning
Symbolizes steadfast love and resilience, thriving even in dry conditions.
Found at
Fences and trellises along academic buildings campus-wide.

Lhuang India

Dendrobium lindleyi · Orchidaceae family

Blooms
February – April
Appearance
Small epiphytic orchid. Golden-yellow flowers with finely fringed petal edges, hanging in clusters.
Meaning
Symbolizes elegance and perseverance, since it grows on host trees without needing soil.
Found at
Attached to large trees in the botanical garden and shaded areas around campus.

Inthanin

Lagerstroemia speciosa · Lythraceae family

Blooms
March – May
Appearance
Medium to large tree with crepe-like purple to pink flowers clustered at branch tips; petals are thin and crinkled.
Meaning
Symbolizes respect and admiration; commonly planted as a shade and ornamental tree across the campus.
Found at
Faculty of Humanities and the walkways surrounding it.
