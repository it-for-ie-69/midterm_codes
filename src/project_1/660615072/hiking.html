Doi Pha Klong

Doi Pha Klong is one of my favorite hiking destinations near Chiang Mai. I visited this place with my friends and enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere, beautiful mountain scenery, and fresh air. The trail is surrounded by forests and offers several viewpoints where you can relax and admire the natural landscape. It is a great place for hiking, taking photos, and spending quality time outdoors with friends.

📍 Location Ban Hmong Doi Pui, Chiang Mai
🥾 Distance Approximately 10–14 km
⏱️ Duration Approximately 5–6 hours
⭐ Difficulty ★★★★☆ – Moderate to Challenging

Huay Tung Tao Waterfall

Huay Tung Tao Waterfall is one of my favorite nature destinations near Chiang Mai. I visited this place with my friends and enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere, beautiful forest, and refreshing waterfall. Instead of taking the regular trail, we chose a different hiking route, which made the journey more challenging and took about two hours longer than expected. Although the hike was more difficult, it became a fun and memorable adventure. The round-trip distance was approximately 6 kilometers, making it a great choice for anyone who enjoys nature and light hiking.

📍 Location Huay Tung Tao, Don Kaeo, Mae Rim District, Chiang Mai
🥾 Distance Approximately 6 km (Round Trip)
⏱️ Duration Approximately 3–4 hours
⭐ Difficulty ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) – Easy

Pha Lat to Doi Suthep Temple

The hike from Wat Pha Lat to Wat Phra That Doi Suthep is one of my favorite trails in Chiang Mai. I completed this hike with my friends, starting from the peaceful forest at Wat Pha Lat and hiking all the way to Doi Suthep Temple. The trail is quite steep and can be physically challenging, but the beautiful forest, fresh air, and the rewarding view at the temple make every step worthwhile. I highly recommend this route to anyone looking for a fun and challenging hiking experience.

📍 Location Wat Pha Lat to Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, Chiang Mai
🥾 Distance Approximately 7–8 km (Round Trip)
⏱️ Duration Approximately 2–3 hours
⭐ Difficulty ★★★☆☆ (3/5) – Moderate
