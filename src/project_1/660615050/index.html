Welcome to my little space sharing the beautiful nature of Chiang Mai University, sitting right at the foothills of Doi Suthep.

Every winter, the campus comes alive with vibrant colors, especially when the iconic Thong Kwao trees burst into flame-orange blooms during graduation season.

This diary is a simple collection of these seasonal blossoms—what they look like, where to find them, and the little moments they color.

  • Thong Kwao is both CMU's and Chiang Mai province's official flower
  • Known in English as "Flame of the Forest" due to its bright orange petals.
  • A deciduous tree, 10–15m tall, with unique 3-leaflet compound leaves.
  • It usually stands in full bloom from late December through March.
