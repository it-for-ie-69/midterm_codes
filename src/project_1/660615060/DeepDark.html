    Horror Games

    I am a kind of person that has really enjoyed horror stories and horror games since primary school. I remember the first horror game I ever played back in those days was Five Nights at Freddy's, which at that time started to be popular with kids around my age, and Scott Cawton, the person behind this game, had already launched Five Nights at Freddy's 2. At first, I thought it was just a horror point-and-click game as usual, but as I grew up, I started to enjoy the stories of the game even more until I became one of the fans of this game that always dive into the stories and lore of this game. (Even though I can't play the game myself and read the official novel anymore due to the upscale nature of the game itself and the price of the novels and the sequence of the books that have so many chapters and spin-offs that I can't count anymore.)
