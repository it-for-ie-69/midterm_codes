How to Make a Very Cute Furby Cake

A friendly step-by-step guide to baking and decorating your very own fuzzy little dessert friend

Prep 1 hr
4 Cakes
Beginner friendly

What you will need

Ingrediants

  • Cold milk cake batter, 1 recipe — or choose any cake base you like
  • Whipping cream, 400 ml
  • Chocolate of your choice, 230 g
  • Fondant, 400 g
  • Food coloring — choose colors as desired
  • Cake decorating sugar sprinkles, as desired

Tools

  • Scissors
  • Plastic wrap
  • Circle cutters in various sizes
  • Piping bags
  • Piping tips
  • Rolling pin
  • Cake turntable, rubber spatula
  • Mixing bowl
  • Spoon

Steps

  1. Bake and level your layers: Bake two round cakes for the body and one small dome cake that will help shape the ears. Let everything cool fully, then level the tops so they stack flat.
  2. Stack and carve the body: Stack the round layers with a thin layer of buttercream between them. Gently carve the top into a rounded, egg-like shape — Furby's body is soft and stout, not sharp-edged.
  3. Shape the ears: Cut the dome cake in half to form two ear shapes. Trim each into a rounded triangle and attach to the top of the head with toothpicks for support.
  4. Crumb coat and chill: Apply a thin layer of buttercream over the whole cake to lock in crumbs. Chill for 20–30 minutes so the surface firms up before the final coat.
  5. Pipe the fur texture: Using a grass or star piping tip, cover the entire cake in short buttercream strokes to mimic Furby's fuzzy coat. Work in small sections, slightly overlapping each stroke.
  6. Add the face: Roll white fondant circles for the eyes and add black fondant pupils. Shape a small triangle from yellow or orange fondant for the beak-like mouth, and place both just below the ears.
  7. Finishing touches: Dust lightly with cocoa powder in the ear creases for depth, add a fondant belly patch if you like, and let the cake set for at least an hour before slicing.
